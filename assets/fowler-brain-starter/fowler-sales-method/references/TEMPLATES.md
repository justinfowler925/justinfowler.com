# Working templates

Version: 0.1.0 · All bracketed values are fields to fill from permitted evidence, not facts.

## Account packet

- Account and exact identity: [name; responsible record reference]
- Audience and permitted users: [access scope]
- Owner and last reviewed: [person; date]
- Current objective and meeting: [decision; date; participants]
- Evidence: [claim; source reference; source date; retrieved date; authority; limitations]
- Approved company claims: [exact supported capability; reference; effective date; scope]
- Hypotheses: [interpretation; competing explanation; test question]
- Conflicts and unknowns: [gap; who can resolve it]
- Live-state references: [CRM/task/document references; snapshot dates if offline]

## Meeting brief

Account: [identity] · Meeting: [date and participants] · Pack: [version]

Decision to advance: [one sentence]

Evidence worth knowing: [up to five sourced points, labeled by authority]

Hypotheses to test: [up to three; each includes a competing explanation]

Discovery questions: [five prioritized questions tied to evidence]

Desired next step: [proposal; confirmed owner/date only if supported]

Unknowns and verification limits: [what the brief cannot establish]

Learning check, when requested: [one question for the rep]

## Discovery summary

Source meeting: [reference and date] · Pack: [version]

Buyer statements: [problem; impact; outcome; process; constraints; source locations]

Changes since prior context: [new evidence; contradiction; unresolved issue]

Commitments: [who; what; agreed date or not agreed; source]

Recommended action: [proposal and supporting evidence]

Proposed record changes: [field; current known value and timestamp; proposed value; source; approval state]

## Session handoff

- Account/work item and permitted audience: [identity and scope]
- Objective: [what remains to accomplish]
- Method version and installed surface: [version; client; project]
- Sources read: [reference; date; relevant conclusion]
- Decisions made: [decision; decision maker; supporting evidence]
- Output artifacts: [actual saved location or explicitly unsaved]
- Confirmed commitments: [owner; action; date; source]
- Open questions and conflicts: [unresolved issue]
- Next action: [one concrete action; owner]
- Tool/access limitations: [missing capability; snapshot freshness]
- Resume instruction: [what to open and verify before continuing]

## Release and surface receipt

- Package: [name/version/digest]
- Maintainer and audience: [owner; public/internal/restricted]
- Changes and superseded version: [summary; prior version]
- Surface and project: [actual destination]
- Tester role and date: [identity class; timestamp]
- Cases executed: [case IDs; results; output artifact references]
- Not executed: [case IDs and why]
- Review decision: [pilot/accepted/rejected; actual reviewer]
- Rollback target: [prior release and installation steps]

## Lesson candidate

Situation: [where this applies]

Observed mistake and evidence: [output/source comparison]

Proposed method change: [specific edit]

Boundary or counterexample: [where this should not apply]

Test case: [input and expected behavior]

Owner decision: [pending/accepted/rejected; date]
