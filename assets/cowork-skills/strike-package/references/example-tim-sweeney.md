# Worked example: Timothy M. Sweeney

This example demonstrates the desired evidence discipline for a hypothetical Clearspeed meeting with Timothy M. Sweeney, Liberty Mutual Insurance’s Chairman, President & CEO. Research cutoff: September 3, 2026.

## Identity resolution

The common name “Tim Sweeney” could refer to multiple public people. The correct subject was matched using:

- current employer and title on Liberty Mutual’s management page;
- appointment chronology in Liberty Mutual’s April 9, 2025 chairman announcement; and
- a professional biography describing a Liberty Mutual career beginning in 1993.

## Example evidence chain

**Verified fact:** Liberty Mutual says it uses AI in claims processing and fraud detection and that decisions are made by people.

**Assessment:** A proposed risk signal should be framed as human-reviewed decision support, with governance and outcome monitoring.

**Question to test:** What evidence, fairness review, and operational controls would a new claims-risk signal need before a controlled evaluation?

The source supports the fact. It does not directly state the assessment or answer the question.

## Example gap statement

“No reviewed public source shows that Liberty Mutual is evaluating Clearspeed, using voice-based risk assessment, or assigning vendor selection in this area to Sweeney personally.”

This negative statement prevents a plausible opportunity hypothesis from becoming a fabricated buying signal.
