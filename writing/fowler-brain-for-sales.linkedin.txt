Our best working methods should travel with our team.

Builders give AI context, reusable procedures, tools, examples, and checks before delivery. Salespeople deserve that working environment too.

I've drafted the standard I think we should build toward with Fowler Brain:

• One maintained source for shared methods.
• Account context scoped to the right people.
• Small, tested adapters for each Claude surface.
• Concrete templates, examples, and quality checks.
• A saved handoff when the session changes.
• A pilot that measures quality, review time, and what the rep actually learns.

The evidence is promising but task-specific. Uploading a folder is the beginning. The test is whether a fresh session can apply the right method to real work—and whether the person can defend the result.

The article explains the position, its evidence, and the setup. The companion kit makes it possible to try.
