The most useful AI skill I’ve built doesn’t create anything.

It asks one question: “Did you check it?”

To my American ear, that once sounded like “I don’t trust you.” What my Eastern European boss meant was simpler: don’t trust your assumptions. Check the thing itself.

So I turned that discipline into Vlad — Final Boss, an adversarial handoff gate that demands a receipt for every “done,” “tested,” and “deployed” claim.

The measured result so far: the latest three gates caught 10 real defects before acceptance. Four were fixed before PASS. Six blocked a production-readiness claim under FAIL.

Not a made-up percentage. Ten documented misses moved left to the last cheap place to catch them.

The story, the deliberately fictional avatar, and the open-source skill:
https://justinfowler.com/writing/did-you-check-it.html

Confidence is a feeling. A receipt is evidence.
