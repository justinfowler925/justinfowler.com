Where developer help would matter

Keep the cast consistent

Persistent rigs and authored scenes

Unreal produced facial animation curves locally. The missing piece is a reusable Maria/Genie cast with a stable set, materials and lighting. I need a practical path from the current stylized characters to editable rigs, with controllable body acting.

Useful proof: the same character and lighting across three different lines and camera angles, with one editable scene and no identity reset.

Make dialogue drive the face

Lip timing and retargeting

Compare MuseTalk and LatentSync against exactly the same speech window. LatentSync runs after the memory and timing repairs, but has not earned replacement status. Audio2Face produces coefficients; a verified mapping to the character rig is still missing.

Useful proof: an aligned before/after clip, normal-speed listening, and a synchronization score with its limitations. Preserve the face, hands, receipt and background.

Diagnose the DubIt binding

Supplied speech is not locked

Two local runs changed the requested words. All 1,344 LoRA pairs mapped, but the target audio began from noise. A separate stage-two audio-freeze patch passed small CPU checks and remains uninstalled; it does not explain or fix the first-stage failure.

Useful proof: a minimal local reproduction that preserves the supplied words and timing. Compare raw generated audio before any original-audio remux.

Stabilize voice and review

Repeatable acceptance

Fish and Qwen produced the requested words. That does not establish speaker identity or a matching performance. Voice changes also change line duration, so the picture must follow the approved stems.

Useful proof: fixed references and transcripts, comparable loudness, identity and prosody review, and regression clips covering the ending as well as the opening.
