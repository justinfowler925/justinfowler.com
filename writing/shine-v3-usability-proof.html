Shine V3: visual proof is not usability proof

I rebuilt the wrong thing: a screen that passed visual checks but made people invent their next step.

Contrast, accessibility, and a familiar kit structure are necessary. They do not prove someone can capture work, understand priority, decide, and see the result.

Shine V3 now binds user-facing objects to its selected Shadcn or Untitled UI reference and executes the primary workflow in a real browser. A decorative control or static dashboard fails.

That does not replace judgment. It makes the minimum judgment falsifiable: the product must let a person finish the job it claims to support.
